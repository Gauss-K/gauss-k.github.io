The first postulate of Complex Relativity puts physical observers in a real Lorentzian geometry. A complex metric shares that geometry’s light cone. The second postulate keeps physical momenta and masses real, with mass-shell constraints compatible with the complex formulation.

## Questions for the programme

What conditions would give the framework consistent dynamics? Which predictions could be compared with measurements and established models? The relation to observed particle content and higher-energy extensions remains part of the research programme.

The [research page](../research.html#postulates) sets out both postulates. The Zenodo record for [Can Light Tell Whether Spacetime Is Complex?](https://zenodo.org/records/24003062) presents the focused investigation.
