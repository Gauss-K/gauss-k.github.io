The novels

Two works of fiction follow the lives behind scientific discovery, from the printing houses of the Renaissance to a future shaped by intelligent machines.

Reading order

The Dreamless Machine is the main novel. To follow the story chronologically, begin with its prequel, The Nameless Box.

Novel

The Dreamless Machine

不会做梦的机器

As physicist Lin Che searches for a deeper understanding of space and time, a machine named Noah enters the lives of scientists and their families. Who bears responsibility for a discovery when a machine can help make it?

Historical prequel

The Nameless Box

没有名字的匣子

A mysterious box passes through the worlds of Leonardo, Galileo, and Newton. Printers, reformers, and ordinary people shape the course of knowledge alongside the figures who enter the history books.

English PDF 中文 PDF

Across the two novels, scientific ideas become part of imagined lives shaped by ambition, institutional power, love, and responsibility.
