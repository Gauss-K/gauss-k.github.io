01 / The question

What would it mean for spacetime to be complex?

Complex Relativity (CR) explores a framework in which complex geometric structures are related to real measurements. The programme begins with a shared light cone and two physical postulates.

The framework is presented as ongoing theoretical research, not as an experimentally established replacement for general relativity.

02 / FoundationsStarting assumptions

Two physical postulates.

Postulate P1

Real observers &
causal geometry

Physical observers use real Lorentzian geometry whose light cone is shared by a complex metric.

Postulate P2

Real momentum &
mass shells

Physical momenta and masses remain real, with mass-shell constraints compatible with the complex formulation.

Research direction

The programme asks how causal structure, real observer measurements, and mass-shell conditions constrain possible complex extensions of spacetime geometry.

03 / Work ahead

Open questions are part of the programme.

The relation to observed particle content, higher-energy extensions, and distinctive experimentally testable predictions remain subjects for further research.
